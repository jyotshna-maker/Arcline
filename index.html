Why it looks like this

Precision. A single stripe is cut from heel to toe, the same line a clean pull shot draws through the air. Nothing else on the shoe competes with it.

Balance. A wide heel base and a flat forefoot platform keep the stance planted before the weight moves.

Explosive performance. The forefoot is shaped to flex early so the push off the back foot feels quick.

Confidence under pressure. Muted materials, one bold accent, no noise. The shoe stays calm so you can too.

Technology, as proposed

Responsive cushioning

Concept

Breathable upper

Concept

Durable outsole

Concept

Lightweight construction

Discipline you can see from the stands

Indian cricket runs on routine: the early nets, the repeated drill, the patience to wait for the right ball. A leader at the crease sets the tempo for everyone else.

That is the idea behind Arcline. Arc Pull One takes its cue from the pull shot, the way it looks relaxed and then arrives all at once. We admire the way Rohit Sharma plays it, and that admiration shaped our brief.
